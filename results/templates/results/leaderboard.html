HOW RANKING WORKS
  1. Each question counts your best scored submission. Your total is the sum.
  2. A higher total ranks first.
  3. If totals are equal, less time to reach your final score ranks first (measured from when you began the exam).
  4. If still equal, fewer scored submissions ranks first.
  5. Participants equal on all three share a rank. Participants with no points share the last rank.
