What is monitored

  • When this tab or window is hidden or loses focus.
  • When you leave fullscreen mode.
  • Attempts to reload, close or navigate away from the exam page.
  • Periods when your browser stops contacting the server.
  • The answers you type in the editor, saved automatically, with the time of each save.

Each event is stamped with the server's clock.

What is not monitored

  • No camera or microphone.
  • No screen recording or screenshots.
  • No keystrokes outside this page.
  • No content from other tabs or applications, and no browsing history.

Please understand

  • This website cannot stop you from switching windows or using other programs. It can only detect some of those events.
  • Recorded events are indicators for human review. They are not proof that anyone cheated.
  • You get one attempt. The timer is kept by the server and starts when you enter fullscreen and begin.
  • You need a desktop browser that supports fullscreen.
